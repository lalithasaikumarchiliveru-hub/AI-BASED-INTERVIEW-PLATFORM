Features

Everything you need
to ace any interview

From behavioural to system design, PrepMind covers it all with real-time AI coaching.

AI-Score Feedback

Scored on Communication, Technical Depth, Relevance, and Confidence with specific improvements after every answer.

Resume-Tailored Questions

Upload your CV and the AI generates questions that reference your real experience — just like a human recruiter.

Job Description Matching

Paste any JD and get your match score, skills gap, and a personalised set of practice questions.

Progress Analytics

Radar charts, trend lines, streak trackers, and personalised weak-area alerts to guide your practice.

Voice Recording
