Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to start?
No. The Free plan requires no card. You can practice 3 interviews per month indefinitely without paying anything.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your Pro or Team subscription at any time from your account settings. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards via Stripe — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and more. UPI and bank transfers are available for Indian enterprise customers.
Is there an annual discount?
Yes! Toggle to "Annual" above to save 25% — Pro drops to $9/month ($108/year) and Team to $37/month ($444/year).
What topics are covered?
Pro covers Behavioural, Data Structures & Algorithms, System Design, Product Management, Leadership, and more. New question packs are added monthly.
How does the recruiter assessment work?
Team admins create a role-specific assessment, share a unique link with candidates. Candidates complete the AI interview without needing an account. Results appear ranked in the recruiter dashboard.
